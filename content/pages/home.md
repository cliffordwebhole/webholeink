# WebholeInk v1

Welcome to **WebholeInk**.

This is a developer-first publishing engine. Using Markdown language to render your written content. 

- No UI's
- No database 
- No drag-and-drop 
- No bloat
- No BS

This is your homepage sample. 

The activated theme for for our sample blog. Is Theme-Light

Several other theme options are available. Theme-Dark and Theme-Classic. More theme designs to come in the near future.  

Edit this page to your liking. 

Be sure to check out the links above in the navigation bar. Everything you need to know is there.

You can download this opensource software on our
[GitHub Release](https://github.com/cliffordwebhole/webholeink/releases) page.

To see this software in full action mode and to learn about our development and updates to WebholeInk visit our [StoreFront](https://webholeink.com)

Trust me — it’s **Clifford Webhole approved**.
